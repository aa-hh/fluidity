# What do privacy law and consent require for collecting contributor data?

Type: research
Status: claimed
Blocked by: none

## Question

What does UK and EU privacy law (GDPR) require to collect contributors' recordings, transcripts and corrections for training a shared model: consent wording, opt-in versus on-by-default, retention, deletion requests (and whether a deleted contributor's data must leave an already-trained model), and handling screen text or third-party names in the data? How do Wispr Flow (training on by default for Free and Pro) and similar apps handle it?

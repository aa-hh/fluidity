# What do retraining and hosting cost?

Type: research
Status: claimed
Blocked by: none

## Question

What does it cost to fine-tune a Parakeet-size model (about 0.6B parameters) on rented GPUs, per run and per month at a realistic retraining cadence? What does it cost to serve a fast cleanup model (about 8B parameters, Wispr Flow's setup) to N users at under 250 ms? Propose a spending ceiling for the proving phase from these numbers.
